Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 13, 2026

Overview

Stringlet is a word search utility that runs entirely on your device. Your privacy is important to us, and we have designed the app to respect it fully.

Data Collection

Stringlet does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically:

  • No personal information is collected.
  • No usage data or analytics are gathered.
  • No cookies or tracking technologies are used.
  • No data is shared with third parties.

Offline Operation

Stringlet works entirely offline. It does not require an internet connection and makes no network requests.

Third-Party Services

Stringlet does not integrate any third-party services, advertising networks, or analytics platforms.

Children's Privacy

Stringlet does not collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please reach out via our support page.
