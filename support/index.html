How to Use Stringlet

  • Type your available letters into the search field.
  • Use ? as a wildcard to match any letter.
  • Tap a letter tile to lock it in position or exclude it.
  • Drag tiles to reorder your letters.
  • Results are grouped by word length, longest first.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Stringlet require an internet connection?

No. Stringlet works completely offline.

How large is the word dictionary?

Stringlet includes over 100,000 words.

What do the tile colors mean?

Normal tiles are included in the search. Highlighted tiles are locked to their position. Disabled tiles are excluded from the search.

Can I use multiple wildcards?

Yes. Each ? matches any single letter independently.
